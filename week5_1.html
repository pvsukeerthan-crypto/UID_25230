The Hindu Trinity

THE CREATOR

Creates the universe and all living beings.

Responsible for the beginning of creation.

Shown with four heads, symbolizing knowledge of the four Vedas.

THE PROTECTOR

Protects and maintains the universe.

Restores dharma (cosmic order) when evil increases.

Takes avatars (incarnations) to protect the world.

THE DESTROYER

Responsible for destruction and transformation.

Destroys the universe at the end of a cycle so that new creation can begin.

Also called as the Cosmic Dancer.
